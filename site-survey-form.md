## 5. Survey Key Points | 勘探注意事项

1. [ ] Concrete roof: Check for repairs, contamination, equipment, light strips, obstacles, parapet wall height
2. [ ] Metal roof: Measure rail spacing, verify sheet profile type, confirm drawings match actual roof
3. [ ] Electrical room: Check for cable trench, confirm client requirements for internal drawings
4. [ ] Structural measurements: Column specs, beam specs, beam span, purlin specs, purlin span
5. [ ] Drone photography: Clear roof images + full project rooftop video
6. [ ] Distribution room: Photograph location and surrounding area
