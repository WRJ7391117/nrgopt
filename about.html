About NRGOpt关于 NRGOpt

A decision tool for Chinese exporters facing EU carbon regulations. We combine carbon policy knowledge with clean energy economics — so you know exactly what it takes to stay compliant.

一个面向中国出口企业应对欧盟碳法规的决策工具。我们把碳政策知识与新能源经济性测算结合在一起——让你清楚地知道，要合规，需要做什么、花多少钱。

Background背景

Why NRGOpt Exists为什么有 NRGOpt

2026 is the year EU carbon regulations went from "future concern" to "present cost". CBAM is charging €75/t. The Battery Regulation is demanding carbon declarations. ESPR is rolling out digital product passports. Chinese exporters need a way to calculate their compliance cost and find solutions — fast.

2026 年是欧盟碳法规从"未来担忧"变成"当前成本"的转折年。CBAM 正在征收 75 欧元/吨的碳关税，电池法要求碳足迹声明，ESPR 在推行数字产品护照。中国出口企业需要一个工具，快速算清楚合规成本，找到解决方案。

⚡

What We Do我们做什么

NRGOpt helps exporters calculate their product's carbon footprint under EU methodologies, estimate CBAM costs, design solar + storage systems to reduce carbon intensity, and compare the economics of compliance vs. paying the carbon tax.

NRGOpt 帮助企业按欧盟方法学计算产品碳足迹、估算 CBAM 碳税成本、设计光伏+储能方案降低碳排放强度，并对比合规成本与直接交碳税的经济账。

🔬

Our Perspective我们的视角

Rooted in China's clean energy industry — we understand both the real cost of solar and storage manufacturing, and the evolving EU regulatory landscape. This convergence is rare, and it's exactly where the market needs it most.

扎根于中国新能源产业——我们既清楚光伏和储能的真实制造成本，又跟踪欧盟碳法规的最新动态。这个交叉点很少人站，但市场最需要的恰恰是它。

🎯

Our Vision我们的愿景

Every Chinese exporter should know their carbon compliance status — and have a clear, cost-effective path to meet EU requirements. NRGOpt makes this possible, one calculation at a time.

每一家中国出口企业都应该知道自己碳合规的现状——并有一条清晰、经济的路径满足欧盟要求。NRGOpt 让这件事变得可能，一笔帐一笔帐来。
