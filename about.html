About NRGOpt关于 NRGOpt

Carbon is a real financial cost — whether from EU CBAM, domestic ETS, or supply chain pressure. We help you measure it, manage it, and find the most cost-effective way to reduce it.

碳排放是你财务报表上一笔真实的成本——不管是欧盟碳关税、国内碳市场，还是供应链压力。我们帮你算清楚、管起来，以最经济的方式降低它。
